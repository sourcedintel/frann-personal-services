Privacy notice

Frann Personal Services is a personal integration for Frann’s own Google account. This notice describes that deployment.

Access and use

With the account’s authorization, the integration can access and manage Gmail messages and supported attachments, calendar information, contacts, and Google Drive file information and content. Account information is used to perform Frann’s authorized assistant tasks, maintain conversational continuity, and record the outcomes of account actions.

Processing and sharing

Selected account content can be sent to the configured AI service providers to perform the requested assistant functions. Account credentials are kept outside model prompts. Local runtime records, action receipts, and protected recovery backups may retain account content or derived information.

This deployment has no advertising or data-sale feature. Account content is not made public by connecting the account. Sending mail or sharing a file is an account action performed through Frann’s authorized account workflow.

Retention and control

The account operator manages stored runtime records and recovery copies. Revoking the application’s Google permissions stops new access; it does not automatically erase previously retained records or backups. Requests concerning retained information can be directed to the contact below.
