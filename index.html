Frann Personal Services

A private integration for Frann’s own Google account.

The integration connects Frann’s assistant runtime to her Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts and Google Drive. It supports reading and managing account information, correspondence, scheduling, contacts and files through the account’s authorized permissions.

This is a personal, single-account deployment. It does not offer public registration or a public account-access service.

Read the privacy notice for how Google account information is processed and retained.
